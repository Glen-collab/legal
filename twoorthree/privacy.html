Two or Three

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

Two or Three is a conversation app for a family or a small group. A parent or leader holds the phone; it reads a prompt aloud and the talking happens at the table. This policy explains what it holds, where that lives, and who can reach it.

The short version: nothing leaves your iPhone except to your own iCloud. Two or Three has no server, no account, no analytics and no advertising. We — the people who make it — never receive anything you put in, and there is no mechanism by which we could.

What it holds

Only what you enter, which is deliberately very little:

  • Who is in your group — the names you type, and each person's date of birth
  • Which topics you have used, so the app can move through them rather than repeating

Dates of birth are stored rather than ages on purpose: an age is wrong within a year and would silently start giving a child prompts written for someone younger. The date is used to work out which register of a question to read out, and for nothing else.

What it does not hold

  • Nothing anybody says. There is no notes box and no recording. What is said at the table is not typed in, not stored and not transmitted — by design, not by omission. A conversation that might be written down is a different conversation.
  • No photos, camera, microphone, contacts or location. Two or Three never asks for access to any of them.
  • No account and no sign-in. There is nothing to create and nothing to forget.

Where it lives

On your iPhone, in the app's own storage, protected by your device passcode and iOS file encryption.

In your iCloud, if you have iCloud enabled, in your account's private database — the same mechanism Apple's own apps use. Apple stores it for you; Apple does not have access to it in a usable form, and neither do we. It syncs between your own devices signed in to your own Apple Account.

If you turn iCloud off, the app keeps working entirely on the device.

Nowhere else. There is no networking code in this app at all.

What we collect

Nothing.

  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No crash reporting
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No third-party SDKs of any kind

We do not know how many people use Two or Three, how often, or who is on your roster. If you email us for support, we have whatever you choose to put in that email and nothing else.

Apple may share aggregate, anonymous information with us about downloads and crashes if you have opted in to sharing that with Apple. That comes from Apple, is not linked to you, and is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

Children

Two or Three is operated by an adult. The parent or group leader buys it, opens it and holds it. A child is present at the conversation, not on the device, and no screen asks a child to tap anything.

A parent enters the names and birthdates of the people in their own family. That information stays on the parent's device and in the parent's own iCloud. It is never sent anywhere, never sold, and never shared.

The app is not in the App Store's Kids Category, because it is not designed for a child to use — it is designed for a parent to use with a child in the room.

Deleting it

Remove a person and their details go with them. Deleting the app removes the local copy; to remove the iCloud copy as well, delete Two or Three's data from iCloud in your iPhone's Settings.

There is no copy on a server for us to delete, because there is no server.

Money

Version 1 is free. There are no in-app purchases, no subscription and no advertising. If that ever changes, purchases will be handled by Apple, we will never see a card number, and this policy will be updated before the version that changes it ships.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
