Spotter

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

Spotter is a gym check-in and client record app for a single gym owner. This policy explains what it holds, where that lives, and who can reach it.

The short version: nothing leaves your iPhone except to your own iCloud. Spotter has no server, no account, no analytics and no advertising. We — the people who make Spotter — never receive your records, and there is no mechanism by which we could.

Who is responsible for what

Spotter is a tool you run. It is not a service we operate on your behalf.

You, the gym owner, are responsible for the information you put into it. The names, contact details, birthdates, emergency contacts, measurements and signed waivers in Spotter belong to your gym. They are your records about your members. If your members ask what you hold about them, or ask you to correct or delete it, that is between you and them — we cannot see any of it, and cannot answer for you.

We are responsible for the app itself: that it stores your records on your device and in your iCloud, that it does not send them anywhere else, and that you can get them out whenever you want.

If you operate in a place with data-protection law — the GDPR, the UK GDPR, a US state privacy act — you are the controller of your members' data and Spotter is a tool you use to process it. You may have obligations of your own, including telling your members what you keep and why. The waiver wording that ships with the app includes a section covering this, as a starting point, but it is an example and not legal advice.

What Spotter holds

Only what you type in or record. That can include:

  • People: name, date of birth, email, phone, postal address
  • Emergency contacts, and for members under 18, a parent or guardian's name and contact details
  • Attendance: which days somebody came in, and at what time
  • Money: what was paid, what was bought, what is owed, sessions remaining
  • Body measurements: weight, body-fat readings and the individual skinfold measurements behind them, when you record them
  • Notes you write about a member, including notes about training or injury
  • Signed waivers: the signature image, the typed name, the date, and the exact wording that was signed
  • Your gym's own settings: session times, waiver wording

Some of this is sensitive — health-related measurements, information about minors. It is held the same way as everything else: on your device and in your iCloud, and nowhere else.

What Spotter does not hold

  • No payment card numbers or bank details. Spotter records that a payment happened and for how much. It does not process payments and is not connected to any payment system.
  • No location. Spotter never asks for or accesses your location.
  • No photos, camera or microphone. Spotter never asks for access to any of them.
  • No contacts. Spotter does not read your iPhone's address book.

Where it lives

On your iPhone, in the app's own storage, protected by your device passcode and iOS file encryption.

In your iCloud, if you have iCloud enabled, in your account's private database. This is the same mechanism Apple's own apps use. Apple stores it for you; Apple does not have access to it in a usable form, and neither do we. It syncs between your own devices signed in to your own Apple Account.

If you turn iCloud off, Spotter keeps working entirely on the device.

Nowhere else. Spotter contains no networking code at all. There is no server to send anything to.

What we collect

Nothing.

  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No crash reporting
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No third-party SDKs of any kind
  • No account, no sign-up, no email address required

We do not know how many people use Spotter, how often, or what is in it. If you email us for support, we have whatever you choose to put in that email and nothing else.

Apple may share aggregate, anonymous information with us about downloads and crashes if you have opted in to sharing that with Apple. That comes from Apple, is not linked to you, and is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

Getting your records out, and deleting them

Export. Settings → Export → Export everything produces a single zip containing every record: people, attendance, payments, sessions, measurements, notes, absences, your timetable, and the signed waivers with the wording each person agreed to. It is plain CSV and text, readable on any computer, and not locked to this app.

Delete. Settings → Delete everything removes all records from the device and from your iCloud. Deleting the app removes the local copy; to remove the iCloud copy as well, use Delete everything first, or delete Spotter's data from iCloud in your iPhone's Settings.

Deletion is immediate and permanent. There is no copy on a server for us to delete, because there is no server.

Children

Spotter is for gym owners and coaches, who are adults. It is not directed at children and children do not use it.

Records about members under 18 are entered by you, the gym owner. Obtaining a parent or guardian's consent to hold that information is your responsibility. Spotter supports this by recording a guardian's name and contact details, and by recording who signed a waiver on a minor's behalf.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
