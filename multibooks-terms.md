## MultiBooks is not tax advice

MultiBooks records what you enter and does not check it. It does not decide what
is deductible, what counts as income, or how a category should be treated. Those
answers depend on your trade, your entity type and the year, and they are for
your accountant or tax preparer.

Nothing in this app is tax, accounting or legal advice, and no figure it shows
is a filing.

## Mileage is recorded in miles

MultiBooks deliberately does not apply a mileage rate or show a dollar deduction.
The rate changes from year to year and whether a trip is deductible at all
depends on your business. What the app keeps is the substantiation: the date,
the distance, where the drive went and what it was for. The calculation is
yours and your accountant's.

## Accuracy

The figures in MultiBooks are the ones you typed. Totals, category breakdowns and
exports are arithmetic performed on your entries — they are only as accurate as
what went in. Review anything before it reaches a return.

MultiBooks is not a backup service. Use Settings → Export to keep your own copy
