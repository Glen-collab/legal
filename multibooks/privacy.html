MultiBooks

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 September 2026

MultiBooks is a bookkeeping app for a small business owner. This policy explains what it holds, where that lives, and who can reach it.

The short version: your books stay on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. MultiBooks has no server, no account, no analytics and no advertising. We — the people who make MultiBooks — never receive your figures, and there is no mechanism by which we could.

One exception, described in full below: when you search for a place or measure a route, that request goes to Apple Maps.

What MultiBooks holds

Only what you enter:

  • Your businesses: the names you give them
  • Transactions: amount, date, whether it is income or an expense, the category, the merchant, how it was paid, and what it was for
  • Mileage: the date, the distance, where a drive started and ended, and its purpose
  • Places you use: names and addresses you have saved, such as a home base or a regular destination
  • Categories and merchants you create

What MultiBooks does not hold

  • No bank or card connections. MultiBooks is not linked to any account at any financial institution. It cannot see your balance or import your statements. It records what you tell it.
  • No card numbers. "Card" as a payment method is a label on an entry, not a card number.
  • No location. MultiBooks never asks for or accesses your device's location. When you measure a route you type both ends yourself.
  • No photos, camera, contacts or microphone.

Where it lives

On your iPhone, in the app's own storage, protected by your device passcode and iOS file encryption.

In your iCloud, if you have iCloud enabled, in your account's private database — the same mechanism Apple's own apps use. Apple stores it for you; Apple does not have access to it in a usable form, and neither do we. It syncs between your own devices signed in to your own Apple Account.

If you turn iCloud off, MultiBooks keeps working entirely on the device.

Apple Maps, which is the one exception

MultiBooks uses Apple's MapKit for two things:

1. Searching for a place — when you type "Kwik Trip" into the from or to field, that text is sent to Apple to find matching places. 2. Measuring a route — when you measure the distance between two places, those two locations are sent to Apple to calculate the driving distance.

This happens only when you actively search or measure. It does not happen in the background and it does not happen when you record an ordinary expense.

These requests go to Apple, not to us. We never see them. Apple's handling of them is covered by Apple's own privacy policy. MultiBooks sends no identifier, no account and nothing about your finances with them — only the text you typed or the two points you chose.

Once a place is saved, using it again is a local lookup. Nothing is sent.

What we collect

Nothing.

  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No crash reporting
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No third-party SDKs of any kind
  • No account, no sign-up, no email address required

We do not know how many people use MultiBooks, how often, or what is in it. If you email us for support, we have whatever you choose to put in that email and nothing else.

Apple may share aggregate, anonymous information with us about downloads and crashes if you have opted in to sharing that with Apple. That comes from Apple, is not linked to you, and is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

Getting your books out, and deleting them

Export. Settings → Export for your accountant produces a single zip with a folder for each business, containing a summary, a breakdown by category, every transaction behind those totals, and the mileage log. It is plain CSV and text, readable in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets, and not locked to this app.

Delete. Removing a business removes its records. Deleting the app removes the local copy; to remove the iCloud copy as well, delete MultiBooks' data from iCloud in your iPhone's Settings.

There is no copy on a server for us to delete, because there is no server.

This is not tax advice

MultiBooks records what you enter and does not check it. It does not decide what is deductible, and it does not apply a mileage rate — mileage is kept in miles, with the date, route and purpose, and the rate is a matter for your accountant. Which categories count as what depends on your business. Please map that out with your accountant or tax preparer.

Children

MultiBooks is for business owners, who are adults. It is not directed at children and holds no information about them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
