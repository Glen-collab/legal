Gym Meets

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026
The short version: Gym Meets has no accounts and no servers. Your gymnast's scores and photographs stay on your iPhone and in your own iCloud, which we cannot see into. The app never reads your photo library — it can only add to it. Location data is stripped from every photograph it stores. We collect nothing, we track nothing, and there are no ads.

Information we collect

None. Gym Meets has no sign-up, no login, no account, and no server of ours for your information to be sent to. We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, or device identifiers. There is no analytics package, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no third-party tracking code in the app.

Where your content is stored

Gymnasts, meets, scores, teams and photographs are stored on your iPhone and sync through your own iCloud account so they appear on your other Apple devices. This uses Apple's CloudKit private database. It is your iCloud account, it counts against your iCloud storage, and we have no access to it. Apple's handling of that data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

If iCloud is unavailable — you are signed out, or out of space — the app keeps working and stores everything locally on the device instead. There is no setting to turn sync off; signing out of iCloud, or turning this app off under iCloud in iOS Settings, has that effect.

Photographs of children

This app exists to keep pictures of somebody's child next to her scores, so this section is the one that matters.

  • The photographs never leave your devices and your iCloud account. They are not uploaded to us. There is no server of ours for them to be uploaded to. No one but you can see them unless you choose to send them to somebody.
  • Location data is removed from every photograph the app stores. A photograph taken on a phone usually carries GPS coordinates, a timestamp, and the camera make and model. Gym Meets re-encodes each image and that metadata does not survive — not in the stored copy, not in the thumbnail, and not in the copies embedded in a season book you share. This is deliberate: a photograph of a child should not tell a stranger which gym she trains at.
  • Nothing analyses the photographs. No face detection, no recognition, no tagging, no machine learning of any kind. They are pictures in a list.
  • No video. Deliberately. Video is heavier, harder to strip cleanly, and a bigger thing to hand around than a photograph.

Camera and photo library

Gym Meets asks for two permissions, and only when you tap the control that needs one:

  • Camera — to photograph your gymnast at a meet.
  • Add to photo library — used only by "save this meet's photos to an album", so you can run Apple's own slideshow and put it on a television.

The app cannot read your photo library. That is not a promise about our intentions; it is what the app is capable of. Adding photographs you have already taken uses Apple's own photo picker, which runs outside the app and hands over only the images you pick — the app is never granted access to the library itself, and it does not ask for it.

Other people's children

If you bring a team into a meet, or photograph a group, you are handling other people's children — their names, their scores, and their faces.

The app treats them exactly like your own: stored on your device and in your iCloud, never sent to us, never published. But two things are yours to manage rather than ours:

  • A season book is a shareable file, and it carries whatever photographs and names are in it. Before sending one to anybody outside your own family, consider who else appears in it. Other parents may reasonably have views about where a picture of their child ends up.
  • If another parent asks you to remove their child, swipe to delete that gymnast and her scores and photographs go with her.

Sharing a season book, and exporting

Gym Meets can turn a season into a PDF and can export everything as a zip of spreadsheets, web pages and plain photographs. Both are created on your iPhone and go wherever you send them using the standard iOS share sheet. Nothing is uploaded, published, or hosted by us, and no link to your content exists on the internet. Once you send a file to someone, what happens to it is between you and them.

Network use

Gym Meets makes no network requests of its own and works fully offline. The only network traffic associated with the app is Apple's own iCloud sync, between your device and your iCloud account.

Deleting your data

There is no account to delete, because there is no account. Everything is removable from inside the app by swiping:

  • A gymnast — her scores and photographs go with her.
  • A meet, a team, or a single photograph.

Deletions propagate to your other devices and to your iCloud account. "Export Everything" will give you a complete archive first if you want to keep a copy. Deleting the app from your iPhone removes the on-device copy; you can remove the remaining iCloud copy in iOS Settings under your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

Children using the app

Gym Meets is a record-keeping tool for a parent or coach, not an app for children to use, and it is not in the App Store's Kids Category. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children — because it does not collect information from anyone at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Because we hold no contact information for you, we have no way to notify you directly — this page is the record.
