## What the app is for

Two or Three reads out a question chosen to suit the ages of the people at the
table. It is a prompt, nothing more. It does not know your family, it cannot
tell when a question will land badly, and it has no idea what any of you are
going through this week.

**You decide what gets asked.** Skip anything that is not right for your group
today. A prompt appearing on the screen is not a recommendation that it be
asked.

## Not counselling, and not pastoral care

Two or Three is not therapy, counselling, medical advice, or spiritual
direction, and it is not a substitute for any of them. If a conversation opens
