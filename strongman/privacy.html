Strongman Contest

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026
The short version: Strongman Contest — shown as "Strongman" on your Home Screen, and called Strongman throughout this page — has no accounts and no servers. It asks for no permissions at all: no camera, no photo library, no location, no contacts. The contests you run stay on your iPhone and, if you leave iCloud sync on, in your own iCloud account, which we cannot see into. We collect nothing, we track nothing, and there are no ads.

Information we collect

None. Strongman has no sign-up, no login, no account, and no server of ours for your information to be sent to. We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, or device identifiers. There is no analytics package, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no third-party tracking code in the app.

Permissions

Strongman requests no permissions whatsoever. It does not use the camera, the photo library, location services, contacts, the microphone, Bluetooth, or tracking. If iOS ever asks you for a permission on this app's behalf, something is wrong — please tell us.

Where your content is stored

The contests, events, competitors, and scores you enter are stored on your iPhone.

They also sync through your own iCloud account so they appear on your other Apple devices. This uses Apple's CloudKit private database. It is your iCloud account, it counts against your iCloud storage, and we have no access to it. Apple's handling of that data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Other people's names

This is the one thing worth reading carefully. Running a contest means typing in the names of the people competing, and those are other people's names, not yours.

Those names are free text. They are stored on your iPhone and in your iCloud account exactly like the rest of the contest, they are never sent to us, and they are never published anywhere. Nothing in the app looks anyone up, matches a name against anything, or connects it to a person beyond the letters you typed.

Two things follow from that, and they are yours to manage rather than ours:

  • Whatever you type is what appears — on your phone, on a television if you connect one, and on any results sheet you share. A first name and a last initial works perfectly well if someone would rather not have their full name up on a wall.
  • If a competitor asks you to remove them, swipe to delete them and their scores go with them. See Deleting your data below.

The wall display

When you AirPlay to an Apple TV or an AirPlay-capable television, Strongman shows a standings board on that screen. This is an ordinary second display: the picture goes from your iPhone to that television and nowhere else. Nothing is broadcast, uploaded, or made available beyond the room. Names and scores on that board are visible to anyone who can see the screen, which is the entire point of a scoreboard, but it is worth saying plainly.

Sharing and printing a results sheet

Strongman can turn a contest's results into a PDF. That file is created on your iPhone and goes wherever you send it using the standard iOS share sheet, or to whatever printer you choose. Nothing is uploaded, published, or hosted by us, and no link to your results exists on the internet. Once you send a file to someone, what happens to it is between you and them.

Network use

Strongman makes no network requests of its own. It is built to work in a basement gym with no signal, and it does. The only network traffic associated with the app is Apple's own iCloud sync, between your device and your iCloud account.

Deleting your data

There is no account to delete, because there is no account. Everything is removable from inside the app:

  • Swipe a contest in the contest list to delete it. Its events, competitors, and every score go with it.
  • Swipe a competitor or an event inside a contest to delete just that.

Deletions propagate to your other devices and to your iCloud account. Deleting the app from your iPhone removes the on-device copy; you can remove the remaining iCloud copy in iOS Settings under your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

Children

Strongman is a scorekeeping tool for people running strength competitions. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Because we hold no contact information for you, we have no way to notify you directly — this page is the record.
